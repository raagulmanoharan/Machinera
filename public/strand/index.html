MACHINERA

A small ritual of noticing and keeping a moment. Each photo becomes a weave, a single rug for that moment.

How it works

Your photo’s colours become yarns, with more influence given to the centre of the frame. Light, contrast and complexity guide a symmetric pattern. The yarns interlace over and under, with shadows and subtle texture bringing the rug to life.

Photos are processed on your device, never uploaded or saved. Only woven memories stay in this browser. Clearing site data removes them.
