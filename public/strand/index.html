THE IDEA BEHIND STRAND

A moment.
One color at a time.

Strand reads every pixel of your image in order, row by row. The overview fits that long sequence into your screen; zoom all the way in to inspect individual pixels.

Your image is processed entirely on your device. The exported JSON contains every original RGBA value, in sequence, with the image dimensions so it can be reconstructed.
