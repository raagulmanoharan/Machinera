Strand turns the colours in your photos into playful digital textiles. Our striped weave takes inspiration from jamakkalam, the vibrant woven floor coverings of South India.

The other patterns explore rippling colour and intersecting threads. These are digital interpretations, rather than reproductions of a traditional artisan’s work.

Your photo stays on your device. Drag to turn your cloth, tap to send a ripple through it, and save the finished weave as an image.
