WEAVE YOUR MEMORIES

MACHINERA

Some moments ask to be kept
in something softer than a photograph.

Gather a little light.
The colours become yarn.
The light, contrast and quiet complexity
find their way into the weave.

Each crossing follows a pattern
drawn from what you saw.
A new moment joins the last,
without unravelling what came before.

Touch it. Let it move.
Scroll through the things you noticed.
A cloth made of looking.
